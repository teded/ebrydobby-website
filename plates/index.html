Plates Family Travel Game

The classic license-plate game, built for your phone.

Spot plates and car logos, check them off, earn points, and keep the whole family watching the road together.

 App Store▶ Google Play

License plates

Play with plates from the U.S., Canada and Mexico. The iOS version also includes Australia and additional specialty plates.

Car logos

The iOS app also lets players track more than 100 car logos from manufacturers around the world.

Saved games & maps

On iOS, save trips, compare games, share statistics, and view where items were found on a map.

How it works

See it. Tap it. Score it.

When someone spots a license plate or car logo, mark it in Plates. Each find earns points, and the game can last for a single drive or continue across a longer trip.

Built for families

Plates is easy to understand, quick to use, and designed for shared road-trip play. Work together as a family or make it a friendly competition.

Languages

Plates supports English, French, and Spanish.
