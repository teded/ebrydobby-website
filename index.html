Privacy by design

Your road trips stay with your family.

Plates does not require an account and does not maintain servers that store your trips, location history, license plate collections, or personal travel data.

🔒

No account required

Play without creating a profile or handing over personal information.

📱

Trips stay on your device

Your saved trip and location information remains local unless you choose to share it.

🧭

You stay in control

Location access can be changed at any time in your device settings.
