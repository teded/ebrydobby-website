Frequently asked questions

How do I start a new game?

Open Plates and begin a new trip or game from the app’s main controls. Your exact options vary by platform and app version.

Can I save a trip?

Saved games are available in the iOS version. Android feature parity is being modernized.

Which countries are included?

Plates includes license plates from the U.S., Canada and Mexico. The iOS version also includes Australia.

I bought content before. What should I do?

Use the app store account originally used for the purchase and look for the restore-purchases option. If you still need help, email support.
